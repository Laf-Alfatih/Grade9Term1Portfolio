First Language English In this term, I progressed my reading skills and abilities by learning reading strategies such as close reading and pre-reading, while refining my skimming and scanning skills
Global Perspectives Work Diving deep into research techniques, resolving conflicts, and learning to share the perspective of the world
History Work Understanding the key concepts of WW1 and the spark that triggered it all. We learn about M.A.I.N (Militerism, Alliances, Imperialism, Nationalism) as well as the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinad and his contribution to WW1
Science Work I worked on a Grade 9 general science project that included topics from chemistry, biology, and physics. It involved using what we learned about things like chemical changes, heredity and ecosystems, and concepts such as force and energy. The task focused on understanding the material and applying it to different questions. Overall, it was fairly easy since the content was based on lessons we had already studied in class.
Physical Education Work Diving into the World of Badminton explores the fast-paced and strategic nature of this exciting racket sport. It highlights key skills such as agility, quick reflexes, and precise shot control, while also emphasizing discipline, focus, and sportsmanship both on and off the court.
Mathematics Work Cambridge Stage 8 and 9 Mathematics focus on strengthening core skills in algebra, geometry, statistics, and number concepts. These stages build problem-solving abilities and prepare students for more advanced math topics and exams.
Arts Work In this term, we explored the art of fonts and typography, creating our own style from several references
Computer Science Work Computer Science is a class where we learn about how computers work and how information is processed. In this term, we studied binary numbers, including how to convert between binary and decimal, and how to perform binary arithmetic such as addition. These topics helped us understand how computers store and calculate data using only 0s and 1s.
Indonesian Work Understanding the process of creating an observational report of Laporan Hasil Observasi to strengthen our understanding of the environment around us.
