Areas of improvement One thing I need to improve is my time management, especially when I only have one or two tasks left to finish. I often work efficiently when I have many tasks because I feel a stronger sense of urgency and motivation to complete them. However, once most of the work is done and only a few tasks remain, I tend to think that I can finish them later. Because of this mindset, those last tasks sometimes remain unfinished.

To improve this habit, I need to maintain the same level of urgency even when only a small amount of work is left. One way I can do this is by setting a personal rule to **always finish every task before stopping**, even if there are only one or two remaining. I can also set small deadlines for the final tasks or treat them as priorities instead of postponing them. By developing this discipline, I can become more consistent and ensure that all my work is completed on time.
